THE STORY BEHIND THE GALAXY

Our Website History

One team, one dream, one universe of possibilities.

🌠 Chapter 1 — A Big Dream

I wanted to create the most beautiful website in the world. Not just an ordinary website, but something unique, creative and unforgettable. I wanted a design that could capture people's attention and make them feel like they were exploring another universe.

My goal was to combine my passion for chess with my imagination and creativity to build something special. The stars were the limit!

♟️ Chapter 2 — Built for My Lichess Team

I created this website for my Lichess team, Galaxy Stellar Chess. I wanted our community to have its own place on the internet, where players could discover the team, meet its members and find information about our tournaments.

This project is more than just a website. It is a way to represent our team, share our passion for chess and give our community its own identity.

Want to join us? Visit our Galaxy Stellar Chess team on Lichess .

💻 Chapter 3 — Turning the Dream into Reality

I built this website using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Little by little, I added pages, navigation, a cosmic background and features to make the experience more interesting.

Every improvement brought the project closer to my vision: a beautiful, original website that reflects the spirit of Galactic Stellar Chess.

🌌 Chapter 4 — Open Source, Endless Possibilities

I share the project on GitHub so people can explore the code and follow its development. The journey is still going, and there is always room for new ideas, improvements and discoveries.

✨ Chapter 5 — The Mission Continues

My ambition has not changed: I want to keep improving this website and make it more beautiful, useful and original with every update.

This is only the beginning. Our team has a universe to explore, and our next move is waiting among the stars.
